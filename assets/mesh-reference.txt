MESH TYPES
──────────
Muscle      Color-coded by weekly set count vs. goal. Uses emissive property.
            Default goal: 10 sets/week (configurable per muscle in settings).

Dynamic     Hair meshes. Color reflects overall muscle progress across ALL muscles.

Static      Fixed appearance. White with 0.4 emissive intensity. Not affected by workouts.


MUSCLE LEVEL COLORS  (applied via mesh.material.emissive)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Level 1 — #C8C8C8  Gray      Less than 2 sets this week (no meaningful activity)
Level 2 — #FAE89A  Yellow    2+ sets, below 1/3 of the way to goal
Level 3 — #F5A0B0  Pink      2+ sets, past 1/3 but below 2/3 of goal
Level 4 — #A8D4F5  Blue      2+ sets, past 2/3 of goal
Level 5 — #E4A8E0  Purple    At or above goal (default: 10 sets)

Level formula (goal G, sets S):
  S < 2              → Level 1
  S >= G             → Level 5
  step = (G-2) / 3
  S >= 2 + step*2    → Level 4
  S >= 2 + step*1    → Level 3
  else               → Level 2


HAIR LEVEL COLORS  (applied via mesh.material.color)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Hair level is determined by the MINIMUM level held by ALL 15 muscles.
Every muscle must reach a level before hair upgrades.

Hair Level 1 — #1A1A1A  Black          Default / not all muscles at L2
Hair Level 2 — #F5C518  Yellow         All muscles Level 2+ (Super Saiyan)
Hair Level 3 — #E8305A  Red            All muscles Level 3+ (Super Saiyan God)
Hair Level 4 — #3A8FD4  Blue           All muscles Level 4+ (Super Saiyan Blue)
Hair Level 5 — #C44BB0  Purple         All muscles Level 5  (Ultra Ego)

HairStatic (index 6) blends: lerp(#9B5FC0, hairLevelColor, 0.5)
  At Hair Level 1 it stays solid #9B5FC0 (no blend).
